About Marita

Marita McVeigh is an artist who works in oil and watercolor painting, cyanotype and printmaking, including etching. Her subjects include flowers, leaves, landscapes and the seashore.

She has shown her work in two featured artists shows at the Upper Dublin Public Library, alongside her son, artist Ed McVeigh.

Every piece on this site is available through her Etsy shop, artworkbymarita.
