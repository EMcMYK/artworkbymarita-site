Hi, I’m Marita.

I started painting in my 30s. I had left my career in international banking to raise my family, and one day I watched Bill Alexander paint on TV. I thought it was some kind of magic. For my 30th birthday my husband gave me a set of oil paints, and as soon as I tried them, I knew I wanted this in my life.

I took adult education classes, where I met the artist Ellen Hall. She encouraged me, and I spent twelve years painting in her home studio and with the Ivyland Art Group. Later I went back to school, and in 2012, at 56, I earned my Associate in Fine Arts from Montgomery County Community College. I fell in love with printmaking there, and I’ve won awards for my etchings and prints.

Oil is my home base, but I love trying everything: etching, linocut, collagraph, pastel, watercolor, and lately cyanotypes, drying in the sun on my clothesline. I’ve taught drawing, painting, pastels and linocut to other artists, too.

Mostly I paint flowers. Each bouquet is unique, but temporary. Painting them keeps them beautiful forever. My paintings feel like wildflowers to me. Each one is different, and the ideas pop up like a garden in bloom.

I like to paint the joyful parts of life with bold colors and lots of expression. Thank you for visiting. I hope something here brings a little of that joy home to you.

A few highlights

  • Associate in Fine Arts, Montgomery County Community College, 2012
  • First Place in Printmaking, MCCC Student Art Show, for the etching “London Sky”
  • Thomas Allan Byrne Memorial Award, Manayunk Roxborough Art Center juried show, for the collagraph “Maritaville”
  • Featured artist at Love Park, Philadelphia, in “Canvasing Philly” (2013)
  • Vendor artist at The Craft Boutique at Moyer Farmhouse, Chalfont, since 2012
  • My art was chosen for a Fox Chase Cancer Center card made to lift patients’ spirits (1999)
