Privacy

This website shows Marita McVeigh’s artwork. It doesn’t sell anything directly, doesn’t ask for any of your information, and doesn’t set cookies.

Buying a piece

Every “Buy on Etsy” button takes you to Etsy, where checkout, payment and shipping happen. What you share there is covered by Etsy’s privacy policy.

Browsing

There are no ads, tracking pixels or analytics on this site. The site is hosted by Cloudflare and its fonts come from Google Fonts. Like any website, they receive basic technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver pages and keep the site secure.

Contact

If you email or message Marita, she uses what you send only to reply to you.
